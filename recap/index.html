One day in Batam. 51 signed up, 23 showed up, 16 builds shipped.
Batam Innovation Center wrote up Hacktoberfest Hack Day on LinkedIn. This is the day from our side: laptops open by noon, lunch next to the terminal, every team on the big screen, and a room full of Arduino, Wokwi and Gemma 4.
Congrats to the four winning teams: Joni Bastian, Dzulfikar Dzulfikar, Kiel Tampubolon, and Ahmad Miftahul Zaki with Aliif Al Ghufroon. Thank you to the judges Muhammad Rizky Syahfie S. and Syam Sarosa.
